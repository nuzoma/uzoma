BizTrack

Know your daily profit in 10 seconds


Role: Product Manager (Team Project)

Context: FinTech · Mentorship Programme Capstone · 2026




Context


The challenge was to conceptualize and pitch a startup solving a real Nigerian problem to international investors.


Problem


We identified a widespread, relatable issue: millions of Nigerian micro-retailers don't know if they're actually profitable. Shop owners rely on notebooks for tracking, spend hours on manual calculations at month-end, and frequently run out of best-selling stock because there's no tracking system to alert them.


Solution


We built BizTrack, a web app designed to replace notebooks and calculators with instant clarity. I worked on the product strategy end-to-end, defining the core problem, designing a solution around three features (log a sale in 3 taps, auto-calculate daily profit, low stock alerts), and shaping the business model.




Key Learning


Solving for underserved users means designing for simplicity first. The best solution replaces chaos (manual tracking of sales and stock) with one clear number. The day's profit.
