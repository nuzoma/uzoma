About Me


A few years ago I had absolutely no idea what product management was. But looking back, I had been developing the instincts for it long before I knew the name.


Growing up, I was the person people came to when something needed fixing or figuring out. Not because I had all the answers, but because I genuinely wanted to help. At the time I was just being helpful. What I did not realise was that I was building empathy and problem solving skills, two things that sit at the core of great product management.


It is different now. I am intentional about it. I think about why products are built the way they are, why certain decisions are made, and what it would take to make things better for the people using them. I do not assume I know what users need. I research, I ask questions, and I let the evidence lead.


As someone with an engineering background, I bring a mix of user empathy, structured thinking, and the ability to understand how things work behind the scenes, not just what users see, but what makes it all hold together.



Core Skills: Product Strategy & Roadmapping, User Research & Empathy, Product Lifecycle Management, Data-Driven Prioritization, Agile/Scrum, Go-to-Market (GTM) Planning.
